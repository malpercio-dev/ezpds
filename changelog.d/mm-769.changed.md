A space credential whose own JWT fails verification now answers the Atproto Spaces reference's name for the failure instead of `InvalidToken`: `BadJwtSignature` for a forged or wrong-key signature or an `alg` that does not match the authority's key, `BadJwt` for a malformed signature, and `BadJwtIss` for an authority whose DID document publishes no `#atproto` key.
