A space-credential read that fails verification now answers the same error names as the Atproto Spaces reference, all 401: `BadSpaceSignature` for a missing or bad HTTP signature, `BadSpaceAudience` for a signature made for another audience, and `BadJwt`, `BadJwtType`, `BadJwtIss`, `BadJwtSub`, `BadJwtCnf`, `JwtExpired` or `CredentialRevoked` for a faulty credential, in place of `InvalidToken` and `ExpiredToken`.
