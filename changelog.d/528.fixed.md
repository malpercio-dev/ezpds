Unlocking an identity now re-registers it for push notifications on the spot, so an identity that was locked when Obsign opened starts receiving sign-in requests right after the unlock instead of waiting for a future launch. Scanning a sign-in QR with the iPhone Camera app (instead of from inside Obsign) now opens the consent approval screen directly — including working out which of several identities the request is for — where it previously just landed on the overview. The server also logs when a sign-in push could not be sent because the account has no registered device.
