`describeServer` now advertises a `spaces` capability so clients and syncers can discover that a deployment serves the Atproto Spaces alpha surface. It is keyed to `signing_key_master_key` — without one, space writes still succeed but the sync routes (`getLatestCommit`/`listRepoOps`/`getRepo`) answer 503, so the capability is withheld. `GET /v1/admin/health` now also reports the space jti-replay and oplog-compaction sweeps' last completed pass.
