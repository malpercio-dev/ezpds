<title>Wallet IA phase 2 — Protection surface + home strip</title>
<description>
Second phase of the mode-based wallet IA (design: `docs/design-plans/2026-07-24-wallet-information-architecture.md` §2.5).

App-level Defend surface, giving the invisible PLC monitor a visible face:

* **Home protection strip**: "All identities secure · checked N min ago" (or alarm state). The strip is a **door**, not just a readout — tapping opens the Protection surface.
* **Protection surface**: every identity's status row (tap-through to that identity's panel/alarm) + **monitor history** — when the background sweep last ran, what it checked, per-identity last-verified times. In alarm state the list reorders by urgency (most urgent first).
* Needs a small IPC addition to expose monitor sweep timestamps/history (the only IPC change in the whole IA plan) — `plc_monitor.rs` currently records nothing user-visible about successful sweeps.

Independent of phases 3–4; pairs naturally with phase 1 (<issue id="86409e5c-e57c-491c-83fc-d9cd68716848" href="https://linear.app/malpercio/issue/MM-465/wallet-ia-phase-1-identity-instrument-panel">MM-465</issue>, shared status vocabulary).
