<description>
Fourth phase of the mode-based wallet IA (design: `docs/design-plans/2026-07-24-wallet-information-architecture.md` §4).

Replace `mode_select`'s door taxonomy (create/import/recover — five sibling journeys with near-synonymous names) with **one situation question** ("Add an identity — what's your situation?"):

1. **Starting fresh** → create flow (did:plc/did:web method choice + `pds_config` capability gate unchanged, one level in).
2. **I have an account somewhere** → import/claim flow.
3. **I lost access to my wallet** → share-recovery flow.
4. **My server is gone** → honest routing, not a flow: identity already in wallet → points at that identity's "Move or rebuild" door; wallet also lost → chains share recovery first, then rebuild (disaster recovery structurally requires wallet custody).

First launch lands on this question directly; with existing identities it's reached from home's "Add identity". All downstream ceremony flows unchanged.
