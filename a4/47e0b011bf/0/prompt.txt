<title>Wallet IA phase 1 — identity instrument panel</title>
<description>
First implementation phase of the mode-based wallet IA (design: `docs/design-plans/2026-07-24-wallet-information-architecture.md`).

Rebuild the per-identity surface as an instrument panel — state leads, actions follow:

* **Status panel** at the top: large-format sibling of `UrgencyBadge` (same four-state safe/warning/critical/expired vocabulary, color+icon+label+position, never color alone). Secure state shows "directory checked N min ago" + a quiet "Security checkup" disclosure; critical state is the alarm entry (existing `alert_detail` → `recovery_override` content hangs off it unchanged).
* **Use zone** directly below: Sign in to an app (OAuth consent/QR), Sign in to Bluesky (app passwords), My agents.
* **"Move or rebuild" door** (own visible entry between Use and Manage): Move to another server (migration flows) + Rebuild from backup (disaster recovery). Credible exit is first-class, not buried surgery.
* **"Manage identity" door**: everyday tier visible on entry (Change handle, Backups = media+posts as one item, DID document view), plus an **Advanced** entry that opens a vestibule — a plain-language framing screen — before the surgery list (Rotate repo signing key, Re-key, Repair hosting endpoint, Remove identity). Existing per-operation gates (biometric, hold-to-confirm) stay; the vestibule is additive framing.
* `DIDDocumentScreen` stops being the hub and becomes the document inspection/verification surface inside Manage identity.

No IPC changes. Screen re-homing map is §7 of the design doc.
</description>
