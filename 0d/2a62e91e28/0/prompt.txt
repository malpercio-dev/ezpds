<title>Wallet IA phase 3 — alarm takeover landing</title>
<description>
Third phase of the mode-based wallet IA (design: `docs/design-plans/2026-07-24-wallet-information-architecture.md` §3).

When an unauthorized change is active and the app opens/foregrounds, land **directly on the alarm surface** — zero navigation between phone-unlock and the one clear action:

* One identity in alarm → its alarm surface (the critical status panel expanded, i.e. today's `alert_detail` content).
* Multiple identities in alarm → Protection surface in alarm mode, most urgent first.
* Always dismissible ("Not now") to a home rendered in alarm state (protection strip becomes a pinned critical banner, affected cards elevated). One clear action *offered*, never a lock-in — calm under alarm.
* **Landing precedence**: an in-progress ceremony resume (recovery epilogue, pending removal tombstone retry, mid-migration) still wins the launch decision — interrupted key-material handling outranks re-showing a known alarm. The alarm banner still renders inside those flows' chrome where safe.

Touches the launch/`visibilitychange` decision ordering in `+page.svelte`; no IPC changes.
