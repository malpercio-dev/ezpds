<title>NSE timeout path renders the unverified notice without recording a breadcrumb — Settings stays green while banners say "couldn't verify"</title>
<description>
## Symptom

Observed live 2026-07-29 during the first production push-to-approve exercise: every push renders the "Couldn't verify a notification from your Custos instance" banner, while Settings → Notifications reports zero recent failures (green). The diagnostics surface exists precisely to explain that banner, and in this state it silently contradicts it.

## Mechanism

`ios/NotificationService/NotificationService.swift` has two paths that render the unverified notice **without** writing a breadcrumb:

1. `serviceExtensionTimeWillExpire()` — the timeout path substitutes the notice and strips the sealed block, but never calls `NotifyBreadcrumbs.record`. Every timed-out push is invisible to Settings.
2. The `mutableCopy` platform-contract guard at the top of `didReceive` (deliberate, documented — probably fine to leave).

There is also a structural blind spot shared by all breadcrumbs: if the extension cannot reach the shared keychain access group at all, the key/pin reads fail (→ notice via `.unverified(.openFailed)`) and the `NotifyBreadcrumbs.record` write fails just as silently (→ green Settings). A fully broken app↔extension keychain contract is therefore indistinguishable from perfect health in the one place built to explain it.

## Fix directions

* Record a `.timedOut` (or equivalent) breadcrumb in `serviceExtensionTimeWillExpire` before substituting the notice — the write is cheap and last-gasp-safe (delete-then-add under AfterFirstUnlock).
* Consider a sentinel the app can check for the keychain-broken case: e.g. the extension writes a tiny "extension ran at <ts>" heartbeat on every invocation; Settings showing "notices rendered but the extension has never checked in" converts the silent contradiction into a named diagnosis. (The app can compare against `UNUserNotificationCenter` delivered notifications carrying the unverified title.)

## Context

Found while diagnosing production pushes that render unverified with green diagnostics on the newest TestFlight build — root cause of that incident may be the timeout path itself or the keychain mismatch; this issue covers the observability gap that made the two indistinguishable.
